A quiz night for two: couple-friendly categories

Ahmed Moaz · 14 October 2026

A quiz night for two works when neither of you owns the categories. Run a trivia quiz with three: one you pick, one your partner picks, and one you agree on. The game deals the trivia questions evenly across the three, so each of you gets a home category and the shared one decides the evening. It also makes a quiz for couples that does not turn into a test of who reads more.

Pick three categories, one each and one between you

Trivia Tavern has nine categories, from Movies & TV to Books & myths, with Sports, Geography, History, Science & nature and Food & drink in between. Leave it on Mixed and everything is in play. For two people I would not. Mixed spreads the draw across all nine, so one of you may meet an evening of the other's best subject by chance.

Choose three. A standard match is thirty questions over three rounds, and the game splits them evenly between the picked categories. In its own test, three very different pool sizes still deal exactly ten questions each.

So the rule is simple. Each of you names a category you know cold. The third is one neither of you claims, and it settles the match.

Can you still win from behind?

Yes, until the last round. The default match pays easy questions 100 points, medium 200 and hard 300, and a ten-question round is four easy, four medium and two hard: 1,800 points at full marks. The last round counts double, so up to 3,600. Nobody should read the table as settled after two rounds.

Add a personal round away from the game

The game cannot take your own questions, so anything personal happens outside it. Write five questions each, about yourselves, with four options, and read them aloud between the match and the break. Score them on paper, one point a right answer. Keep the questions kind. A quiz night is not an exam on someone's memory of your first date.

Mix the two in whatever order you like. I would play the match first, then the personal round, because the match has a clock and the personal round does not.

How long does a quiz night take for two people?

About thirty-five minutes. The game lists a match at fourteen minutes, a personal round takes about ten, setup about five, and the rest is talking. A question closes once both of you have answered, so two players usually finish ahead of the full clock.

The first three are fixed by the game or by you. The talking is the part you cannot plan.

Make it a standing date night

Rotate who picks the first category each time, and keep a running score on paper. After a month the shared category may become the one you are both a little afraid of, which is the point. The same table seats up to eight, so the next evening can be with friends, trivia online from two sofas or one. Trivia Tavern, the game used in this plan, is free to open on Boardit, with no download. If the scores end level, the game plays sudden death between the tied players, so nobody has to argue about it.
