About

I am Ahmed Moaz, a developer in Egypt. I build Boardit, a small site of six free multiplayer browser games.

These notes are mostly about the engineering behind browser games: keeping a hidden word hidden, testing a game engine with thousands of seeded games, and the small decisions that make a page honest to search engines. Some notes are about the history and numbers inside the games themselves.
