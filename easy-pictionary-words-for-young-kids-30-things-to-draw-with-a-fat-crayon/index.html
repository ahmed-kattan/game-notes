Easy pictionary words for young kids: 30 things to draw with a fat crayon

Ahmed Moaz · 5 November 2026

The easiest pictionary words for young kids are things they touch every day. A four-year-old does not need a hint to know what a boot is, and they can scribble one with a fat crayon in the time it takes a grown-up to read the card. This list has 30 easy pictionary words for young kids, in five groups of six, written for the small hands that will draw them. The word Pictionary belongs to another company and has nothing to do with my site. It is simply what parents type when they want a list.

Things you wear

  1. boot
  2. slipper
  3. hoodie
  4. shorts
  5. raincoat
  6. beanie

Clothes are a good place to start because every child owns them. A boot is a tube with a flat bottom, and a slipper is the same tube cut short. If a child draws a hoodie as a big square with a hood on top, that is a perfectly good hoodie.

Bits of me

  1. foot
  2. ear
  3. knee
  4. elbow
  5. toe
  6. tongue

A child can draw these on themselves, in the air or on the grown-up's knee. For a very young child, let them point first and then draw. The guesser can touch their own ear and say the word.

At the table

  1. jar
  2. kettle
  3. straw
  4. lid
  5. pot
  6. tray

Each is a plain kitchen shape with one extra feature: a handle, a spout, a rim. Ask the child to show where the handle goes, and the picture is done.

Animals with simple shapes

  1. ant
  2. turtle
  3. mouse
  4. hen
  5. goat
  6. lamb

A lamb is a cloud with four legs. A turtle is a dome with a head. An ant is three dots. These are the rare animals that a very young child can draw almost as well as an adult.

Bedtime and bath

  1. pillow
  2. blanket
  3. towel
  4. soap
  5. bucket
  6. sponge

Last, a calm group for the end of the day. These are all soft or round, and a child can draw most of them with a single curved line.

What makes a word easy for a small child?

I used three tests. The child must see the thing every week, so they have a picture in their head already. It must be one object, not a scene. And its name should be short enough to say in one breath, so the guesser can shout it. A word that passes all three, like "jar", gets drawn quickly and guessed quickly. A word that fails one, like "farmyard", belongs in a later list.

How do you play with such young children?

Take away the clock. A small child needs time to look at the card and decide where to begin. The grown-up guesses, out loud and slowly, and gets a few wrong on purpose so that the child has the pleasure of correcting them. After a few rounds, swap, and let the child guess your drawing, which can be deliberately simple.

There is no scoring to keep. Cheering at the end of each picture is enough.

What about the online version?

The game itself is for older players. Boardit's terms say it is not made for children under 13, so the children in this age group should draw on paper. An older cousin or a parent who wants to play with friends on a screen can try Boardit's Draw and Guess, a free drawing game online for two to twelve people, where the host can add a private list of words.

Ready for the next list

When your child draws a sock in a few strokes and names every item here, move on to words with two parts, such as a fish bowl or a toy train.
