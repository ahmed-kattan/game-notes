Funny pictionary words from everyday situations: 24 small disasters to draw

Ahmed Moaz · 31 October 2026

The funniest drawings in a game are usually of a situation, not an object. A cat is easy. A person locked out of their own house in pyjamas is a story, and a story drawn in twenty seconds goes wrong in ways that make a table howl. That is the idea behind this set of funny pictionary words, made for a drawing game with friends or a family evening. Each one is a small disaster, an embarrassment or a moment everyone recognises. I wrote the list fresh for this article.

Why do situations get the biggest laughs?

An object has a standard picture, and the first guess is usually right. A situation needs a cast, a setting and a problem, and a drawer who has twenty seconds has to leave most of it out. The table fills the gaps wrongly, and wrong guesses are where the laughter lives.

There is a second reason. A situation is something every player has felt. You need no film or band to know the horror of waving back at someone who was not waving at you.

Which situations happen at home?

  • Locked out in pyjamas
  • Stepping on a toy in the dark
  • Dropping a phone in the sink
  • Burning the toast
  • Sneezing while holding a full cup
  • Forgetting why you walked into a room
  • Sitting on the remote
  • Finding a spider in the bath

Home situations are the gentlest, and they suit a family group.

Which situations happen out in public?

  • Walking into a glass door
  • Waving at a stranger
  • Trapped in a revolving door
  • Parallel parking with an audience
  • Running for a bus that drives off
  • Lift doors closing on a friend
  • Spilling a drink on the person in front
  • Slipping on ice in front of a crowd

These are quick to sketch because they have one clear moment. Show the door, the crowd and the red face.

Which situations happen at social events?

  • Forgetting someone's name
  • Hugging a person who wanted a handshake
  • Laughing in a silent room
  • Saying "you too" to a waiter who said enjoy your meal
  • Singing happy birthday off key
  • Wrong group chat
  • Reply all
  • Arriving a day early to a party

Social slips suit a table of adults. They land harder when everyone has been there.

What is the method for a twenty-second sketch?

Draw the victim first, big, with a face. Then draw whatever causes the trouble, and add one clue for the setting, such as a sink or a door. Skip the background. If there is time, add a speech bubble with a mark in it, not a word, because writing the answer is against the rules.

How does the game treat a terrible drawing?

A guess scores more the earlier it comes, from 500 points for an instant guess to 100 for the last second, and the first right guess keeps all of its speed points. If nobody gets the word, the drawer scores nothing, and the drawing goes into the gallery at the end. That gallery is where a bad drawing earns its place in the evening.

On a list like this, I would leave the hints on. New rooms open with three, and each one turns a dash into a letter while the seconds fall away. A long situation shown as a row of dashes is easier to crack with a couple of letters in place.

Where can you try the list?

You can use it on paper, with a timer. For an online round with friends, a host types the words into the custom list and the players never see the list in advance. A room of this online drawing game holds up to twelve, and guests give a name and nothing else. The same list can be typed in as custom words in the draw and guess game at Boardit, the free site where I publish my games.

One last tip

Start with the home situations to warm up, move to the public ones, and keep the social slips for the final round when the table is loose.
