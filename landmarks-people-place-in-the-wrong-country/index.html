Landmarks people place in the wrong country

Ahmed Moaz · 15 October 2026

These eight landmarks are easy to place in the wrong country, which makes them good raw material for trivia questions: Kilimanjaro is in Tanzania, the Matterhorn sits on the border of Switzerland and Italy, Victoria Falls lies between Zambia and Zimbabwe, Neuschwanstein Castle is in Germany, Petra is in Jordan, Angkor Wat is in Cambodia, Chichen Itza is in Mexico and Machu Picchu is in Peru. If you are writing a trivia quiz for friends, here is what the sources say about each, and how to turn the fact into a fair question.

Where a border or a photo blurs it

Four of these sit near a line on the map, or are photographed from the wrong side of one. They make good geography trivia because the mix-up is built in.

Which country is Kilimanjaro in?

Tanzania. The mountain stands there, at 5,895 metres. A photo caption on its Wikipedia page reads "Kilimanjaro from Amboseli National Park, Kenya", so the page itself shows a view of the mountain taken from Kenya.

Which country is the Matterhorn in?

Both Switzerland and Italy. The summit is not on one side of the line but on it, on the border between Switzerland and Italy. Zermatt is on the Swiss side and Breuil-Cervinia on the Italian. Edward Whymper led the first ascent on 14 July 1865.

Which country is Victoria Falls in?

Two of them. The Zambezi River forms the border, so the falls belong to both Zambia and Zimbabwe. They are about 1,708 metres wide and roughly 108 metres high. David Livingstone named them in 1855, and the local Lozi name, Mosi-oa-Tunya, is still in common use.

Which country is Neuschwanstein Castle in?

Germany. The castle is in Bavaria, near Füssen, and its Wikipedia page describes it as near the border with Austria. Ludwig II of Bavaria had it built, and the foundation stone was laid on 5 September 1869.

Where nothing blurs it

I have no survey of which landmarks people really get wrong, so I picked these for a different reason: the sources show a border or a viewing spot that makes the mix-up easy. Four more are on the list because they are famous and the answer is clean.

Which country is Petra in?

Jordan. Petra stands in the Ma'an Governorate near Wadi Musa. It was the capital of the Nabataean Kingdom and became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985.

Which country is Angkor Wat in?

Cambodia, near Siem Reap. It was built in the early twelfth century for King Suryavarman II, and a depiction of it has been on the Cambodian flag since 1863.

Which country is Chichen Itza in?

Mexico, in the state of Yucatán. The Maya built it, and it was named one of the New Seven Wonders of the World in 2007.

Which country is Machu Picchu in?

Peru, in the Cusco region, at an elevation of 2,430 metres. The Inca Empire built it in the fifteenth century, and Hiram Bingham brought it to international attention in 1911.

Turning a fact into a fair question

A good general knowledge question about places gives a player something to reason with, and it suits a trivia night because the answer is short. In multiple choice trivia the form is simple: name the landmark, ask for the country, and offer four answers where each wrong one is a believable neighbour. Choosing the wrong answers is the craft. For Petra, Israel, Saudi Arabia and Syria all share a border with Jordan. For Angkor Wat, Thailand, Laos and Vietnam all share a border with Cambodia. Neighbours make believable options without being unfair. Do not put the answer in the stem. Do not ask for a year or a height unless the number is the story.

One to try

Here is a question I wrote for this post. It is not taken from any game.

Mount Kilimanjaro is often photographed from Amboseli National Park in Kenya. In which country does the mountain itself stand?

  • A. Kenya
  • B. Tanzania
  • C. Uganda
  • D. Ethiopia

The answer is Tanzania. Ask it online with friends or at a table, and see how many people go for the country in the photo caption. That count is the only survey you will get here.
