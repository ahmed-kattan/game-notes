Letter hints in a drawing game: what zero to three hints do

Ahmed Moaz · 8 November 2026

A hint in a drawing game fills in one letter of the word at a time. In Draw and Guess the host chooses how many letters can be revealed, from none to three, and the default is three.

I wanted to see what that does to a word, so I tried the arithmetic.

The idea

Say the word has eight letters. With no hints, the guessers see eight blanks. With one hint, one letter is shown and seven are blank. With three hints, three letters show and five remain. The fraction revealed goes from 0 to 12% to 38%.

A short word changes more. A word of four letters with three hints is mostly given away: three letters show and one is blank. For short words, three hints turn a drawing into a spelling test.

Why it matters for the drawer

The drawer is paid the average of the speed points of the others. A guess that comes fast earns more for the guesser, and the drawer gains too. Hints help guessers who are stuck, so they raise the number of correct guesses.

When to change it

  1. For children, leave three hints on. The letters make the game easier to enjoy, and a child who is stuck is not left out.
  2. For a competitive table, try zero or one. The drawing has to do the work, which rewards good drawers.
  3. For a mixed group, keep two. Enough to help, not enough to give the word away.

Other settings that go with it

The host can also change the drawing time (40, 60, 80, 100 or 120 seconds), the rounds (2, 3, 5 or 8), and the number of words to choose from, between two and five. A small number of words speeds up the pick. Five gives a drawer a real choice.

Twelve seconds to choose

Hints work alongside a choice made before the drawing starts. The drawer has 12 seconds to pick a word, and if they do not, one is picked for them. With the default five words on offer, that is a fast scan for the one you can draw, and the hint letters then help the guessers after the pick.

The short version

Hints are the easiest dial for the difficulty of a table. Start with the default and move it down as the group improves.
