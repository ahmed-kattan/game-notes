One link to six games: how a guest joins in a few taps

Ahmed Moaz · 9 November 2026

The easiest way to lose a game night is to make joining hard. Boardit tries to make it easy: one link, a name, and you are in. These are the steps.

For the host

  1. Open the site and pick a game. There are six: Ludo, Snakes and Ladders, Property Rush, Trivia Tavern, Draw and Guess and Who's the Spy.
  2. Create a table. It opens in a lobby. By default a new table is public and shows in the list of open tables. If you want it invite-only, tap the padlock.
  3. Share the link. There is a copy button and a share button. The table also has a five-character code.
  4. Choose the settings. Every game has its own list, and the defaults work if the host skips it.
  5. Start when everyone is in.

For the guest

  1. Open the link. It takes you to the table.
  2. Type a name. A guest needs no account, no email and no password.
  3. Wait in the lobby. You can see who is there.

What guests keep

A guest's name and record are kept for 30 days. A player who makes an account keeps more, with levels and rewards for their character.

If something goes wrong

Every table and lobby shows a connection signal: green, yellow or red. If your connection drops, you are told, you can retry, and your seat is held.

Who can fit

The games differ. Seat counts differ by game: Ludo four at most, Property Rush six, Snakes and Ladders and Trivia Tavern eight, Draw and Guess twelve, and Who's the Spy eight online with a minimum of three.

Where to start

Boardit is the site with all six games, free and with no download.

What a guest does not need

A guest types a name and nothing else. A name is enough. If a friend is nervous about joining something new, that list is usually what reassures them.

How many games were recorded

The link is how most tables start. In the first six days of recordings, 1 to 6 November 2026, the site recorded 205 games: 14 of Draw and Guess, 69 of Property Rush, 36 of Ludo, 31 of Snakes and Ladders, 45 of Trivia Tavern and 10 of Who's the Spy. Each of them began as a table that somebody opened.

What a first-timer needs

A link and a name. Everything else can be explained at the table.
