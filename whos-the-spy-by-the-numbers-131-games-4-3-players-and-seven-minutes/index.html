Who's the Spy by the numbers: 131 games, 4.3 players and seven minutes

Ahmed Moaz · 13 November 2026

When I started to look at the table log, Who's the Spy surprised me. It has the fewest games of the six, and the biggest tables. These are the numbers, from 13 September to 30 October 2026, counting only games with at least two real people.

The figures

MeasureWho's the Spy
Games with real people131
Finished games110
Average real players4.3
Median length7.1 minutes

The other five games had between 179 and 246 games, but their tables were smaller: 2.4 to 3.1 people on average.

What this says

Spy is not played alone or in pairs, because it needs three or more. When a group does gather, it is the largest group on the site. A Spy night is a party game by nature.

A game of seven minutes is quick. The default is three rounds, so each round runs about two and a half minutes on average.

Why short

The vote ends a round, and clues are limited: 20 characters, 25 seconds. A table of four gives eight clues before the first chance to call a vote, at most 200 seconds. After that the vote itself takes up to 45 seconds. Add the reveal and a round fits in a couple of minutes.

How to plan

  1. Three players: a short game, because the vote comes sooner.
  2. Four to six: the middle of the log, about seven minutes.
  3. Seven or eight: longer. More clues come before the vote.

The caveat

A median is an average of games that happened to be finished. A group that plays three rounds of long debates will be over seven. I mention the number as a guide for planning.

Five players, two laps

Tables average 4.3 real people, so five players is a typical table, and at five players two laps of clues take at most 250 seconds. Add the vote and the reveal and the seven-minute median is easy to explain: the clock rarely runs out.

Why the largest tables

My explanation is simple. A hidden-role game gets better with people. Most of the tables on the site are two friends playing Snakes and Ladders. The tables for Spy are the nights when friends came together on purpose.
